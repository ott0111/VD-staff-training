Module 1

Ticket Types

Your first and foremost responsibility as a mod is to efficiently manage the ticket system in Void Esports. We operate with two primary ticket categories, each requiring specific handling protocols.

General Ticket

Created by community members for support inquiries, moderator assistance, or reporting issues. Handle with professionalism and follow escalation procedures for complex matters.

  • Greet professionally
  • Identify the issue clearly
  • Escalate complex cases
  • Document important details
Roster Ticket

Application channel for users seeking to join Void Esports. Initial response must include age verification and direction to appropriate resources. Follow the structured onboarding workflow.

  • Age verification first
  • Ask what role they want
  • Request the correct evidence
  • Ping correct department
Module 2

Core Protocol

Protocol (Non-Negotiable)

All ticket responses should begin with a professional greeting and an age inquiry. Direct new applicants to the appropriate channels and maintain detailed records of all interactions.

Examples of professional greetings

Hello, how may I help you? Hello, what can I assist you with today? Hello! What are you applying for today?

Examples of age verification

May I ask how old you are? What is your current age? Before we continue, what is your age?
Do NOT say: “Yo”, “Hi bro”, “sup”, “bet”, “lmao”, or anything informal in tickets.

Module 4

Mod Responsibilities & Commands

Warn / Report

Use *warn user-id reason exclusively in #staff-commands for minor rule violations. For major or repeated infractions, use /report with detailed documentation.

LOA (Leave On Application)

When unavailable due to illness or personal circumstances, use /loa with clear duration and reason. Prior notification to senior staff is mandatory for extended absences.

Module 5

Ticket Handling Guidelines

General Guidelines

  • Always be professional.
  • Never use informal responses in tickets.
  • Ask what role they are applying for.
  • Always verify age.
  • Ping the correct department when needed.

Role-Specific Instructions (Quick Reference)

@Creative Academy / @Main Creative
Request 2–3 clips, then ping Creative Department.
@Academy Roster
Request tracker + image of default dance in lobby.
@Semi-Pro / @Pro Roster
Request tracker + default dance image, then ping Operations.
@VOID VFX / @VOID GFX
Request portfolio/previous work, ping GFX/VFX Lead.
@Content Creator
Request social links, ping Content Department.
@Coach
Ask how many coached + request links to player trackers.
@Void Grinder+
Offer Grinder+. Request screenshots: creator code “Team.Void”, name includes “Void”, and Discord tag.

Closing the Ticket + Blacklist Format

Closing Script

Final Steps:

Perfect! Please change your name to have “Void” in it and send a screenshot of using our code!

After they send images:

Thank you! Here are your roles! Have a nice day!

⚠️ Blacklist Format

Use this exact format:

User: (Username of the person) User ID: (Discord ID) Reason: (Example: Underage)

Important

Always maintain professionalism throughout the entire interaction. Document all steps and escalate to senior staff when necessary.
